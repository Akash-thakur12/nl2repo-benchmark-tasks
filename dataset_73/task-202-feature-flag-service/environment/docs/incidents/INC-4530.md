# INC-4530 - traffic above the rollout total was being served the last variant

Status: resolved in v3.0. Referenced by flag-spec.md §2.4 and §4.3.

## What happened

Half-ramped flags are normal: an operator allocates 30% `on` / 30% `off` and
leaves the remaining 40% for later. Under v2 the rollout clamped - a bucket
above the running total was served the **last** entry of the rollout list, with
reason `RULE_ROLLOUT`. That silently gave 40% of users a variant nobody had
allocated to them, and it swallowed the rules underneath: a second rule that
would have matched was never consulted, because the rollout rule had already
"decided".

From v3.0 unallocated traffic is not a rollout result. The rule is not decisive
and evaluation continues with the remaining rules, then the default (§2.5). A
rollout that sums to 10000 is unaffected: every bucket is allocated.

## Decision records

Exported verbatim from the audit log. The capture is flag `partial`, salt
`s-partial`, rules in this order:

```json
[{"attribute": "plan", "operator": "in", "values": ["pro"],
  "rollout": [{"variant": "on", "weight": 3000}, {"variant": "off", "weight": 3000}]},
 {"attribute": "country", "operator": "in", "values": ["IN"], "variant": "blue"}]
```

`default_variant` is `off`.

```json
{"request": {"flag": "partial", "user": "u1", "plan": "pro", "country": "IN"},
 "bucket": 822,  "decision": {"variant": "on",   "reason": "RULE_ROLLOUT"}}
{"request": {"flag": "partial", "user": "u2", "plan": "pro", "country": "IN"},
 "bucket": 3209, "decision": {"variant": "off",  "reason": "RULE_ROLLOUT"}}
{"request": {"flag": "partial", "user": "u3", "plan": "pro", "country": "IN"},
 "bucket": 9071, "decision": {"variant": "blue", "reason": "RULE_MATCH"}}
{"request": {"flag": "partial", "user": "u4", "plan": "pro", "country": "US"},
 "bucket": 7481, "decision": {"variant": "off",  "reason": "DEFAULT"}}
```

u1 and u2 fall inside the 6000 that is allocated and are served by the rollout.
u3's bucket is above the total, so the first rule is **not** decisive and the
second rule is consulted - it matches, and the decision is `RULE_MATCH`/`blue`,
not `RULE_ROLLOUT`/`off`. u4 is also above the total and matches no later rule,
so the evaluation falls through to `default_variant` with reason `DEFAULT`.

Under v2 all four were `RULE_ROLLOUT`, with u3 and u4 served `off` - the last
entry in the rollout list. The buckets themselves did not change; only what the
service does with a bucket the rollout does not cover.
